Recorded sounds the game plays. All CC0 (public domain): no attribution required.

- moo-1.mp3, moo-2.mp3, moo-3.mp3: cow moos from BigSoundBank (Joseph Sardin), #2383 and #2386 "Cow moos" and the first moo of #0546, trimmed and level-matched. https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html
- zombie-1.mp3 … zombie-5.mp3: zombie moans from OpenGameArt, all CC0: 1-2 "Zombies Sound Pack" (artisticdude, zombie-16/17), 3 "Zombie noises and moans" (ianzazz, zombienoise1), 4-5 "Zombie moans" (Darsycho, two moans cut from the file); trimmed and level-matched.
- chew-1.mp3 … chew-3.mp3: soft chewing, three stretches of BigSoundBank #0720 "Mouth open" (CC0). bite-1.mp3 … bite-3.mp3: crisp bites, three from BigSoundBank #0407 "Eat a Rusk" (CC0).
- giggle-1.mp3 … giggle-4.mp3: children laughing, for the gnomes (played slowed, muffled and echoing): 1-2 BigSoundBank #1660 "Laughter of Children", 3 BigSoundBank #0980 "Laughing Boy of 16 Months", 4 OpenGameArt "Group giggling"; all CC0.
- troll-1.mp3 … troll-4.mp3: troll grumbles (1-3 idle noises, 4 a roar) from OpenGameArt "Big scary troll sounds" (Darsycho), CC0; used for trolls and frost trolls, and faster for orcs.
- drink-1.mp3, drink-2.mp3, drink-6.mp3: drinking, "Drink_01", "Drink_02" and "Drink_06" from OpenGameArt "202 More Sound Effects" (OwlishMedia), CC0; 1-2 for any water, 6 (then a sip) for a fountain.
- splash-1.mp3 … splash-3.mp3: a hand dipped in the sea (the overworld's edge), splash_04, splash_08 and splash_02 from OpenGameArt "40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX" (rubberduck), CC0; level-matched, the tails faded.
- deep-1.mp3 … deep-3.mp3: the deep things' gurgles, BigSoundBank #1026 "Gurgling sink" (1) and OpenGameArt "10 Slimewater/Monsterwater" (StarNinjas, slime 9 and 5); CC0.
- pig-1.mp3 … pig-3.mp3: the pigmen, BigSoundBank #1658 and #1659 "Grumpy pig" and #1691 "Prout'cochons"; CC0.
- snarl-1.mp3, snarl-2.mp3: the lizards, OpenGameArt "Monster snarls" (Darsycho), CC0.
- saw-1.mp3: a few saw strokes, BigSoundBank #0016 "Saw"; clank-1.mp3, clank-2.mp3: two hits of an axe on a steel wedge, BigSoundBank #0706 "Ax on axe wedge"; for crafting. CC0.
- lava-loop.mp3, boil-loop.mp3: loops of BigSoundBank #0491 "Big broth in a pot" and #0149 "Boiling water", slowed to 60 % and 55 % (lava). CC0.
- fire-loop.mp3: an 8 s seamless loop of OpenGameArt "Fireplace Sound Loop" (PagDev), CC0: the Fire Realm.
- glass-1.mp3 … glass-3.mp3: a glass tapped with a knife, BigSoundBank #0471; chimes-1.mp3, chimes-2.mp3: BigSoundBank #0040 "Dreamcatcher"; the Ice Realm's tinkles. CC0.
- ghost-1.mp3 … ghost-5.mp3: OpenGameArt "Ghost Monster Voice Moaning & Growling" (qubodup); crow-1.mp3, crow-2.mp3: BigSoundBank #3464 "Carrion Crow"; gate-1.mp3, gate-2.mp3: BigSoundBank #0683 "Grinding metal gate"; the Graveyard. CC0.
- kobold-1.mp3 … kobold-3.mp3 (bug_05, bug_08, weird_06), felem-1.mp3, felem-2.mp3 (breath_02, roar_05, fire elementals); ielem-1.mp3, ielem-2.mp3 are unused (ice elementals tinkle with glass-* and chimes-* instead): OpenGameArt "80 CC0 creature SFX #2" (rubberduck). CC0.
- armor-1.mp3, chain-1.mp3, chain-2.mp3, metal-1.mp3 … metal-3.mp3 (armor-light, chainmail1/2, metal-small1-3): OpenGameArt "RPG Sound Pack" (artisticdude); clash-1.mp3 … clash-3.mp3: OpenGameArt "20 Sword Sound Effects" (StarNinjas, sword_clash 1-3); the knights. CC0.
- fire-roar.mp3: 1.8 s of BigSoundBank #0987 "Big Branching Fire" roaring up and dying down, with felem-1/2, for the fire elementals. CC0.
